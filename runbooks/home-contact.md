# For the person at home (printable, no passwords)

<Your name> may call and ask you to check a few things while they're away. You don't need to know anything about computers. You never need a password for any of this.

**Home contact:** <first name> (walked through this once on <date>)

## What's what

- **The Mac**: <describe it: "a small, flat, silver box about the size of a sandwich", and where it sits>. Its small light is on the front.
- **Any drive plugged into it.** Never unplug or move it.
- **The router** (Wi-Fi box) and the **modem** from the internet company.
- **The UPS / inverter**, which keeps these running in a power cut.

## If they call

1. **Is there power at home? Is the UPS/inverter on?** Say what you see.
2. **Router lights**: is the internet light on? If asked, switch the router and the modem off at the wall, wait 30 seconds, switch them on, and wait 3 minutes.
3. **The Mac's light**: on or off? If off and they ask, press its power button **once**. <Where it is: on a 2024-or-later Mac mini it's **underneath the box, at the back**; slide a finger under the back edge.> Don't turn the box over, and don't press it again.
4. **If a screen connected to it asks for a password**, don't type anything. Tell them.

## Never

- Unplug the Mac, the drive or any cable unless asked.
- Type any password you weren't given in person.
- Install or click anything on the Mac's screen.
